Our Septic Services

Comprehensive septic consulting—from tailored system design and permitting through inspections, compliance reviews, and system modifications.

Septic System Design

Every property is unique. We develop septic system designs tailored to site conditions, soil characteristics, local regulations, and project requirements. Our designs are prepared to meet state and local approval standards while supporting long-term system performance.

Services include

  • New septic system design
  • Residential and commercial projects
  • Replacement system design
  • System modification and expansion plans
  • Construction-ready design packages
  • Regulatory compliance documentation

Septic Permitting & State Applications

Navigating septic permitting requirements can be complicated. Tierra Septic manages the application process from start to finish, helping clients obtain approvals efficiently and with minimal delays.

Services include

  • State septic permit applications
  • Permit package preparation
  • Documentation review
  • Assistance with approval requirements

Our goal is to streamline the permitting process and keep your project moving forward.

Septic System Inspections

We provide detailed septic inspections for a variety of situations, helping property owners understand the condition and compliance status of their systems.

Inspection services

  • New construction inspections — Verification of installation and compliance with approved plans
  • System modifications & additions — When adding living space, bedrooms, or square footage that may impact septic capacity
  • Repair evaluations — Assessment of systems experiencing operational issues or requiring repairs
  • Abandonment & sewer connection inspections — Documentation for septic abandonment and municipal sewer connections
  • Compliance inspections — Evaluation of existing systems against applicable regulations and permit requirements

Compliance Reviews

We evaluate existing onsite sewage treatment and disposal systems to determine regulatory compliance, identify deficiencies, and recommend practical paths to approval or remediation.

System Modifications & Expansions

When your project changes—additional bedrooms, increased flow, or site redevelopment—we help plan and document system modifications and expansions that meet Florida requirements and protect long-term performance.
