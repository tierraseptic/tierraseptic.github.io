Soil & Site Testing

Proper soil evaluation is the foundation of every successful septic system. We perform soil and site assessments to determine whether a property is suitable for onsite sewage treatment and disposal.

Soil Evaluations

Analysis of soil characteristics that influence septic system performance, including profiling and classification according to 10YR Munsell color standards used in Florida site evaluation protocols.

Site Suitability Assessments

Evaluation of environmental and site conditions—setbacks, water table indicators, limiting layers, and topography—that affect septic feasibility and system selection.

Septic Feasibility Studies

Determining the most appropriate septic solutions based on site conditions, anticipated use, and regulatory requirements—before you invest in design or construction.

Why soil testing matters

Accurate soil and site data reduce permitting delays, prevent costly redesigns, and ensure your system is matched to real-world site conditions. Tierra Septic provides documentation suitable for permit applications and professional project planning.

  • Munsell soil analysis (10YR) per Florida evaluation standards
  • Clear reporting for builders, buyers, and permit reviewers
  • Experienced environmental health professional on every project
